# Drip watering (Vivosun drip kit + Athom plug)

The Vivosun drip kit's pump is plugged into an Athom smart plug running
ESPHome. Home Assistant turns the plug on and off, using the garden beds'
soil probes and the same plant and season settings as the bed cards. The
pump is on/off only and waters every line at once. Each line's manual valve
sets how much its bed gets, so the dashboard tells you which valve to turn.

Files: `esphome/drip-pump.yaml` (the plug), `packages/drip.yaml` (the logic,
generated by `tools/garden.py`: settings `DRIP_ZONES`, `DRIP`, `EC_FLUSH_ABOVE`).

## Set up

1. **Plug.** In the ESPHome Device Builder, `drip-pump.yaml` is ready to
   install (Athom plugs ship with ESPHome, so adopt the plug and install
   over Wi-Fi). Add it in Home Assistant when it's discovered. Check that the
   entities are `switch.drip_pump_switch` and `sensor.drip_pump_power`; if
   not, change `DRIP_ZONES` in `tools/garden.py` and re-run it.
2. **Package.** Add `drip: !include ha-dashboard/packages/drip.yaml` under
   `packages:` (see [install.md](install.md)) and restart Home Assistant.
3. **Prime the lines.** Press the plug's button, or tap **Devices → Drip
   watering → Water now**, and set each line's valve so its emitters drip.
4. **Turn Auto on**: tap the pump icon on any bed card (left of the pot), or
   **Auto watering** on Devices. It starts off.

## What it does

Every 5 minutes it looks at the beds on the pump:

| Step | Rule |
|------|------|
| Which bed | The **driest bed against its own Dry level** decides. These are the levels on the bed cards, so they already follow the plant, indoor/outdoor, day length, air temperature and soil temperature. A probe reading 0% (out of the ground) is ignored. |
| When | When that bed drops below **Dry**. After a run it keeps topping up (run, soak 45 min, look again) until the bed reaches the middle of its Dry–Wet range. |
| How long | **Missing moisture × minutes per 1%**, 1–8 min a run. Minutes per 1% starts at 0.5 and is **learned**: 45 min after each run it checks how much the bed rose. |
| What time | Outdoors **5–9 AM**, and also **5–7:30 PM** in a heat wave. Indoors **7 AM–7 PM**. A bed far below Dry (under 70% of its Dry level) waters any time from 6 AM to 9 PM. |
| Holds off | Any bed on the pump is already **Wet** (it would get water too). **Freezing** outdoors (air ≤ 38 °F or soil ≤ 35 °F). **Raining**, or **rain forecast** in the next 6 hours (unless a bed is very dry). **30 min a day** used up. |
| Salty soil (EC) | An outdoor bed whose EC is above its plant's level gets one longer run (×1.5) per day, to wash salts past the roots. It's off for indoor pots unless `flush_indoors` is set. |
| Stops itself | **Problem** if 3 runs in a row don't raise the moisture (empty reservoir, kinked or clogged line, probe out of place), if the pump draws under 1 W while on (unplugged or dead), or if no probes are reporting. A Home alert appears. Fix it, then switch Auto off and on. |

`sensor.drip_1_plan` holds the decision: **OK**, **Water**, **Watering**,
**Waiting**, **Paused**, **Limit** or **Problem**, and the `reason`
attribute says why in plain words, e.g. "Bed 2 is dry (25%), waters at 5 AM".
Hold the pump icon on a bed card to see it. While Auto is on, a dry bed
doesn't raise the "needs water" alert unless the pump is stuck or hit its
daily limit.

## Safety

- The plug turns itself off after **15 min**, however it was turned on, even
  if Home Assistant or Wi-Fi is down (`max_run` in `esphome/drip-pump.yaml`).
  Home Assistant enforces the same limit too.
- After a power cut or reboot the plug comes back **off**.
- Pumps are turned off when Home Assistant starts, in case it restarted
  mid-run.

## Setting the valves

The pump can't give one bed more than another; only the valves can. On
**Devices → Drip watering**:

- **Last run, per bed**: how much each bed rose after the last run, e.g.
  "Bed 1 +6%, Bed 2 +1%". A bed that barely rises needs its valve opened
  (or its probe moved nearer an emitter).
- **Valves**: when one bed sits near **Wet** while another is near **Dry**,
  it says which valve to turn down and which to turn up. Change one valve a
  little at a time and give it a day or two.

The learned **minutes per 1%** follows the driest bed. After big valve
changes it re-learns within a few runs, or you can set it by hand.

## Tuning

Edit `DRIP` in `tools/garden.py`, then run `python3 tools/garden.py` and
reload YAML. The settings are commented there. The ones you're most likely
to change are `pulse_max`, `daily_max`, `soak`, the watering `windows`, and
`EC_FLUSH_ABOVE` once you've seen what your probes read in healthy soil. A
second pump is a second entry in `DRIP_ZONES` with its own beds (copy the
Devices section for `drip_2`).
